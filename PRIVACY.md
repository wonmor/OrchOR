# Privacy Policy — Orch-OR Visualizer

_Last updated: October 31, 2026_

Orch-OR Visualizer is an educational app that simulates the Penrose–Hameroff Orch-OR theory of consciousness on your device.

**We do not collect any data.** The app has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The simulation runs entirely on your device, and the settings you choose stay in the app's local storage on that device. Nothing is transmitted, stored on servers, or shared with anyone.

The app does not use cookies, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: john@orchestrsim.com
